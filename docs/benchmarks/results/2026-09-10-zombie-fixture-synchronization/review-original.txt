## Why the stronger premise is sound

The old regression released its injected error as soon as the thread leader became Z. That condition does not establish whole-process shutdown, which is the prerequisite for bypassing monitor failure. The new `isOnlyZombieThread` predicate requires both State Z and `Threads: 1` in the real status record.

A zombie leader cannot return to userspace. With no other remaining thread, a live worker cannot continue allocating or create a new thread. This establishes the intended stable zombie premise without copying or invoking production's per-task PF_EXITING scanner. Requiring the conjunction, rather than a grace interval, preserves the original 2000ms synchronization deadline and 3000ms supervisor timeout.

Applying the predicate to both ENOENT/ESRCH and stale-status exit 0/2 cases is appropriate: both families previously assumed leader Z alone meant process exit. The test now injects its controlled error/stale snapshot only after the condition needed by its expected clean result is true. Production remains fail-closed when a real worker is still running. This repairs synchronization, not expected outcomes or resource enforcement.
